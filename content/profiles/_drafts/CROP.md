## The old story

Poultry Crop Operations functions as the sprawling agricultural leviathan of the district. Abandoning the romanticized
history of independent farming the company rolls across the rural outskirts actively acquiring thousands of struggling
family operations and merging them into deeply centralized and brutally efficient mega facilities.

By relentlessly streamlining the entire cultivation and livestock process Poultry Crop successfully floods the territory
with incredibly cheap and abundant calories keeping the sprawling citizenry fed for mere pennies. However the actual
agricultural harvest is effectively a massive break even operation used entirely as a functional cover story. The true
financial genius of the firm lies directly on Glasswater Row where their executives operate the company as the largest
unregulated land bank in the district.

The firm intentionally chokes out small farmers, forces them into bankruptcy and then quietly buys up all the acres of
prime rural real estate. Once they own the dirt they aggressively manipulate local zoning laws and flip the cleared
properties to massive commercial developers or industrial titans for staggering markups. Because the company generates
astronomical revenue through predatory land speculation while maintaining the flawless public optics of a humble food
provider the stock acts as a highly resilient and deeply deceptive defensive anchor.
