## The old story

Falconet Motor Group began its existence as the deeply unprofitable vanity project of an eccentric, speed obsessed
district billionaire. Originally functioning as a bespoke garage crafting singular hyper-cars for the founder's personal
amusement, the firm has since mutated into a sprawling, vertically integrated automotive leviathan. Today, Falconet is
renowned for producing technologically flawless, highly advanced commuter vehicles at a price point so aggressively
affordable it defies traditional manufacturing logic.

However, Glasswater Row understands that this affordability is not a philanthropic gesture, but a ruthlessly calculated
act of predatory pricing. Falconet intentionally sells its mass-market fleet at a staggering operating loss, utilizing
the vehicles as a physical Trojan horse to systemically bankrupt competing automakers. Once the car market is entirely
capured by the Falconet ecosystem, the firm quietly monetizes their existence through inescapable telemetry harvesting,
algorithmic insurance premiums, and proprietary software-locked maintenance tolls.

To fund this massive daily cash burn and maintain its infrastructural stranglehold, Falconet relies on its 'Apex
Division', a hyper-exclusive luxury tier reserved strictly for global ultra net worth individuals, corporate oligarchs,
and the district elite. By engineering severe artificial scarcity and utilizing the psychological leverage of a Veblen
good, Falconet sells these elite vehicles at extraordinary markups. The astronomical profit margins extracted from the
ultra wealthy perfectly cross subsidize the predatory baseline fleet. Because its entire survival depends on this
delicate, hyper-leveraged equilibrium, the stock operates with extreme polarity. It captures massive, defensive market
share during retail expansions, but remains terrifyingly vulnerable to sudden, violent drawdowns if a macroeconomic
liquidity crisis ever forces the district's billionaires to momentarily pause their luxury consumption.
