## The old story

Talon Credit operates as a ubiquitous financial services firm commanding a massive consumer credit card network and an
inescapable payment processing gateway. While they present themselves as the friendly facilitators of everyday commerce,
Glasswater Row respects Talon as a highly weaponized transaction tollbooth. The company extracts a mathematically
inescapable fraction of a penny from millions of daily district transactions.

However, the true financial genius of the firm lies in its aggressively predatory lending division. Rather than driving
consumers into outright bankruptcy, Talon deploys cutting edge behavioral algorithms designed to mathematically maximize
the lifetime value of every borrower by trapping them in a perpetual cycle of rolling minimum payments. Even when these
algorithms occasionally fail and overleveraged consumers inevitably default, the company attempts to suffer absolutely
zero consequences. Talon immediately packages this highly toxic unsecured debt into bewilderingly complex derivatives.

This initiates a perpetual and fiercely hostile game of cat and mouse with Shrike Standard Ratings. Because Shrike
operates as an unbribable and terrifyingly meticulous arbiter of the market, Talon employs armies of quantitative
engineers whose sole purpose is to mathematically obfuscate the radioactive nature of these assets just enough to slip
past the auditors. When Talon successfully tricks the rating agency and offloads the garbage onto unsuspecting pension
funds, the company prints massive profits. Because they continuously generate massive transaction fees while desperately
trying to outrun their own credit risk, the stock operates as a highly aggressive cyclical powerhouse. It surges
violently during roaring economic expansions, only suffering sudden and terrifying price shocks if Shrike suddenly
catches on to the scheme and issues a catastrophic downgrade.

## The old chief executive's quote

> We don't want you bankrupt. We want you paying the minimum, forever.
