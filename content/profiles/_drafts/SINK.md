## The old story

Sinking Shore Extraction operates as an independent and ruthlessly efficient energy company engaged exclusively in the
exploration, development, and production of raw crude oil and natural gas. Operating primarily in treacherous deep water
offshore blocks and challenging coastal basins, the company utilizes advanced seismic imaging and aggressive directional
drilling techniques to tap into complex subterranean reservoirs.

The firm serves as the absolute brute force harvester of the raw energy required to fuel the relentless expansion of the
district's corporate machine. Sinking Shore openly despises clean energy optics and actively deploys armies of corporate
lobbyists to quietly crush any environmental regulations that might threaten their massive drilling margins. It exists
solely to drag hydrocarbons out of the deep ocean floor and sell them to the highest industrial bidder at absolute
maximum markup.

As a pure play upstream operator, the financial performance and massive capital expenditure cycles of the company are
violently leveraged to global energy demand and commodity spot prices. When the district's economy is booming and the
industrial giants are consuming endless power, the stock skyrockets with immense beta. However, it suffers massive and
terrifying drawdowns the absolute second that global oil prices unexpectedly collapse.
