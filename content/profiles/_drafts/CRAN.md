## The old story

Crane Medical Network operates as the ubiquitous and premier healthcare provider for the entire district. Rather than
focusing purely on medical innovation, the true brilliance of Crane lies in its absolute mastery of bureaucratic billing
and insurance arbitrage.

The company commands armies of algorithmic compliance officers whose sole purpose is to maximize the coding of every
single patient interaction, ensuring that every routine consultation and minor procedure extracts the absolute maximum
allowable payout from corporate insurance retainers and municipal tax pools. They do not deny care. Instead they provide
aggressively excellent care and then trap their institutional payers in a labyrinth of inescapable invoices and endless
arbitration.

Because human biology completely ignores macroeconomic cycles, the stock acts as a profoundly defensive anchor. It
trades with an incredibly low beta and steadily prints cash, only experiencing sudden positive price jumps when
politicians announce massive new mandatory health spending packages that Crane will inevitably find a highly efficient
way to absorb.
