## The old story

Iron Beak Heavy Industries stands as the undisputed physical architect of the entire district. In a financial ecosystem
completely obsessed with abstract algorithms and digital data, Iron Beak operates purely in the brutal reality of poured
concrete and reinforced steel. To call them a construction company fails to capture their absolute monopoly over the
physical world.

If a structure exists within the territory, Iron Beak probably built it. Their sprawling fabrication yards and endless
fleets of heavy machinery are responsible for paving the endless miles of interstate highways, erecting the towering
glass skyscrapers that house the corporate elite, and forging the colossal ocean going supertankers that export raw
capital to the globe. The firm employs generations of laborers who, dedicating their existence to maintaining the
staggering physical momentum of the district.

However, despite its intimidating size, the company is not truly independent. Tightly controlled by its majority
shareholders the institutional conglomerates LAKE and SWAN the company functions effectively as a captive builder for
the financial titans of the region. Whenever Lakebird Bank funds a massive new civic project or Black Swan Capital
orchestrates a sweeping real estate buyout, Iron Beak is automatically awarded the exclusive and highly lucrative
development contracts. Glasswater Row treats the stock as the ultimate macroeconomic bellwether. Because its operations
require astronomical upfront capital expenditure just to keep the dry docks pumping and the cranes moving, Iron Beak
trades as a fiercely cyclical asset. Its share price surges violently on the thermals of regional economic expansion but
suffers terrifying drawdowns the absolute second that infrastructure spending freezes.
