## The old story

Canvasback Logistics commands an immense fleet of ground delivery trucks, long haul trailers, and regional sorting
warehouses. Acting as the physical connective tissue for the digital commerce boom, the company operates an absolute and
unyielding monopoly on the coveted last mile of delivery.

Glasswater Row adores Canvasback because the firm treats its massive logistics network less like a public service and
more like a highly lucrative extortion racket for retail businesses. They do not offer next day delivery as a friendly
consumer perk. They use it as a weapon against independent retailers, forcing massive digital commerce giants and local
vendors alike to pay exorbitant surge pricing and algorithmic routing tolls or face immediate commercial obsolescence.

Because the entire retail economy completely relies on their physical execution, the daily parcel volume flowing through
Canvasback warehouses serves as a perfect real time gauge of global consumer health. For investors, the stock trades as
a perfectly correlated proxy for the broader economy. It scales aggressively during massive retail booms when the
company can ruthlessly hike its delivery tolls, but it suffers immediate drawdowns the absolute second that retail
spending begins to cool.
