## The old story

Tickbird Data Systems is an indispensable financial technology firm that holds the entire market hostage to the truth.
The central premise of their business model is terrifyingly simple. You cannot trade a stock if you do not know the
exact price of it. Operating as the central nervous system of global finance, Tickbird provides the ultra low latency
market data, live algorithmic analytics, and execution platforms used by every major institutional trading desk.

While billionaire fund managers and aggressive investment banks like to believe they control the market, they are
rendered completely blind the second Tickbird unplugs their monitors. Because the company provides the literal oxygen of
the financial sector, it extracts aggressively recurring subscription fees from global institutions that have absolutely
no choice but to pay the toll.

However, maintaining this ironclad monopoly requires constant and ruthless warfare. Tickbird fights tooth and nail
against emerging technology disruptors using every clean and dirty trick in the corporate playbook. The firm routinely
executes predatory acquisitions to swallow smaller rivals before they can pose a legitimate threat. When buyouts fail,
the company is notorious for deploying armies of corporate lawyers to bury competitors in frivolous patent litigation or
intentionally degrading data feed compatibility to freeze out rival third party platforms.

This fiercely protected economic moat grants the company incredibly reliable revenue streams regardless of whether the
broader market is crashing or soaring. Because investors recognize this ultimate pricing power, Tickbird trades at
massive technology multiples, making the stock highly susceptible to sudden valuation corrections whenever broader
technology selloffs punish overly expensive companies.

## The old chief executive's quote

> Every microsecond is a micro-monopoly.
