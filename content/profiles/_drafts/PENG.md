## The old story

Penguin Computing is an aggressive and incredibly high growth technology firm manufacturing elite high performance
hardware and liquid cooled supercomputer clusters. In a society entirely governed by ruthless algorithms and real time
financial surveillance, the sheer mathematical friction generated by the corporate sector produces terrifying amounts of
physical heat.

Penguin provides the essential icy infrastructure required to keep the sprawling data centers of Tickbird Data Systems
and the ruthless credit algorithms of Stork Consumer Credit from literally melting down. They specialize in submerging
overheating processors in cutting edge liquid coolants, allowing the digital brain of the district to operate at maximum
capacity without interruption.

Trading with massive and aggressive volatility, the stock is highly susceptible to viral technology hype cycles. It
operates with a staggering beta and frequently gaps up violently on mere rumors of new microchip breakthroughs or
massive institutional hardware contracts, making it a highly speculative favorite among retail day traders.

## The old chief executive's quote

> The district runs on friction. We sell the ice.
