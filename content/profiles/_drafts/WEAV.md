## The old story

Weaver Marketplace operates as the absolute apex of digital retail within the district. Presenting itself as a
frictionless storefront for everything a citizen could ever need, the company functions as a hyper efficient digital
ecosystem. Millions of independent vendors are forced to sell their goods on the platform because it is the only place
consumers actually shop.

Once a vendor becomes successful, the proprietary algorithms of Weaver meticulously analyze their sales data, Weaver
then deploys its massive capital to aggressively acquire their operations or lock them into highly lucrative exclusive
manufacturing contracts. The company absorbs the best products into its own pristine fulfillment network, instantly
scaling local success stories into district wide phenomenons. The platform is mathematically engineered to predict and
stimulate specific consumer desires using massive troves of harvested data, ensuring the exact goods people want are
always available for instant delivery.

Because its revenue scales directly with absolute consumer volume, the stock trades with a very high beta. During
economic booms, Weaver prints unimaginable wealth as the population gorges on digital consumption. However, the stock
remains acutely vulnerable to sudden inflation shocks or wage stagnation, suffering steep drawdowns the moment the
consumers is forced to scale back their purchasing.
