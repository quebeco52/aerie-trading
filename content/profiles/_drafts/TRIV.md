## The old story

Three Rivers Manufacturing is an incredibly vast and diversified industrial conglomerate where hundreds of different
chemical, medical, and manufacturing divisions converge.

From specialized adhesives and abrasives to medical films and highway traffic signs, the ubiquitous products of the
company are stitched into the absolute fabric of daily life.

Because it owns so many disconnected businesses, Three Rivers moves like a massive and unsinkable cargo ship. It has
incredibly low baseline volatility and almost completely neutralizes any localized market shocks.

## The old chief executive's quote

> Diversification is the ultimate defense mechanism.
