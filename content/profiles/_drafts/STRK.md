## The old story

Stork Consumer Credit is a massive consumer finance lender specializing in unsecured personal loans, auto financing, and
point of sale credit. While its cheerful branding claims the company delivers purchasing power and financial freedom to
the middle class, the internal reality is far more clinical.

Stork operates at the absolute bleeding edge of consumer risk where human stories and personal hardships do not matter
at all. The only thing the company truly cares about is the cold mathematical certainty of a three digit credit score.
Its entire underwriting department has been replaced by ruthless proprietary algorithms that instantly approve or deny
billions of dollars in daily credit based entirely on those exact numbers.

This hyper efficient model allows Stork to print staggering profits during roaring economic expansions when employment
is high and consumers are spending freely. However, because it issues massive amounts of unsecured debt, the stock is
extremely sensitive to macroeconomic data and operates as a hyper cyclical gauge of middle class financial health. When
unemployment ticks upward and those sacred credit scores begin to slip, Stork is completely exposed. The stock crashes
violently at the first sign of rising loan defaults, suffering terrifying drawdowns as the algorithms finally realize
the money is never coming back.
