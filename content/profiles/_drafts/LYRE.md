## The old story

Lyrebird Media operates as the premier advertising and perception management conglomerate of the district. While massive
technology monopolies harvest the raw behavioral data of the population, Lyrebird purchases that exact data to weaponize
it. They do not simply run marketing campaigns. They manufacture absolute societal consensus.

Operating from sleek and utterly pristine creative fortresses, the firm employs armies of behavioral psychologists,
neuroscientists, and algorithmic artists to craft flawlessly targeted psychological operations. When a catastrophic
industrial disaster strikes a heavy manufacturing giant, Lyrebird is immediately deployed to flood the digital airwaves
with soothing counter narratives and perfectly engineered distractions, effectively rewriting reality before the public
can even panic.

They are the ultimate corporate spin doctors, acting as the absolute first line of defense against any brand destroying
scandal. Because every single megacorporation in the district considers their services a mandatory survival expense,
Lyrebird extracts astronomical monthly retainers. The stock acts as a uniquely resilient asset. During roaring economic
booms, they print money from standard product advertising. However, during terrifying corporate crises and massive
public relations disasters, their profit margins actually explode as desperate companies blindly throw billions at them
to quickly sanitize their public image. The only true threat to their stock price is a sudden and catastrophic leak of
their own internal communications, which would shatter the delicate illusion they sell to the world.

## The old chief executive's quote

> We don't report the news. We synthesize reality.
