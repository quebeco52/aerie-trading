## The old story

Cascade Refining and Marketing operates a sprawling network of complex high conversion oil refineries and massive
logistics terminals. While they efficiently manufacture the gasoline and aviation fuel required to keep the district
moving, their financial department operates like an elite quantitative hedge fund.

Cascade is notorious for exploiting inventory accounting loopholes and strategic physical reserves to manipulate the
mathematical crack spread. By rapidly shifting millions of barrels of unrefined crude between offshore shell
subsidiaries, they create artificial paper shortages that completely fool the algorithmic trading bots of Glasswater
Row. This allows Cascade to consistently buy oil futures at a massive discount and sell refined product futures at a
staggering premium without ever changing the actual physical output of their refineries.

Because they extract their massive margins directly from the chaotic inefficiencies of the financial system rather than
gouging the captive commuter at the pump, the stock trades as a steady and highly defensive industrial asset.
