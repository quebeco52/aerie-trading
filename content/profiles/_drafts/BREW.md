## The old story

Copperhead Coffee Roasters projects the welcoming image of a friendly neighborhood cafe, but Glasswater Row understands
the firm is actually a highly aggressive real estate and addiction syndicate. The core product is simply a vehicle for
their true business model, which involves ruthlessly leveraging their massive cash reserves to buy up prime commercial
real estate, completely pricing out independent competitors, and gentrifying entire city blocks.

Their exceptionally sourced coffee is engineered with the exact caffeine profile required to create deep and unbreakable
chemical brand loyalty among the exhausted corporate workforce. Copperhead operates with terrifying pricing power.
Because caffeine addiction is completely immune to economic recessions, the company routinely hikes its prices during
periods of high inflation without losing a single customer, allowing them to extract a mathematically inescapable daily
toll from millions of citizens.

For investors, the stock acts as a magnificent defensive anchor in the consumer staples sector. It trades with an
incredibly low beta and prints staggeringly consistent cash flows, only experiencing sudden negative volatility when
global commodity shortages or aggressive unionization efforts temporarily threaten their astronomical profit margins.
