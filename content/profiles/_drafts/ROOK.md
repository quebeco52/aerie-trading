## The old story

Rook Proprietary Trading operates as the relentless and hyper aggressive engine of daily liquidity within the district.
Unlike the venerated quiet of traditional asset managers, Rook is a deafening and unforgiving arena of high pressure
speculation. The firm functions as a sprawling digital coliseum for elite quantitative developers and ruthless day
traders who thrive purely on absolute market friction.

Built entirely on immense volume and microsecond volatility, the company provides its trading desks with massive amounts
of highly leveraged capital and proprietary algorithmic terminals to scrape fractional pennies off millions of daily
transactions. The internal culture is notoriously brutal and the employee turnover rate remains catastrophic. Those who
cannot survive the punishing intraday pace or who accidentally blow up their trading books are immediately escorted out
by security and replaced by the next morning.

However, for those possessing the raw predatory instinct to navigate the chaos, Rook offers limitless wealth and an
unfiltered connection to the true mechanics of the market. Because the firm relies entirely on the chaotic churn of
daily trading volume rather than deep structural investments, its own stock acts as a highly volatile speculative
vehicle, experiencing violent intraday swings tied directly to the sheer panic and greed of the broader economy.
