## The old story

Bird Power Inc. operates as the regulated electric utility monopoly that constitutes the inescapable physical and
energetic foundation of the Aerie Autonomous District. While public relations campaigns emphasize civic reliability and
subsidized baseload power for retail consumers, the utility's true profit engine is a masterpiece of statutory capture:
the ruthless exploitation of mandated rate-base mechanics and guaranteed return-on-equity covenants.

Under the District’s archaic Grid Resiliency Ordinances, Bird Power is legally mandated to maintain an astronomical
spinning reserve margin to prevent system-wide blackouts. Because utility regulators guarantee a statutory 9.8% return
on all approved capital expenditure, Bird Power deliberately gold-plates the power grid—erecting massive surplus
generation capacity, advanced substations, and high-voltage transmission lines. Municipal authorities and corporate
industrial parks are legally bound by 'take-or-pay' capacity tariffs, forcing them to subsidize this immense energy
surplus regardless of actual consumption. When generation exceeds the capacity of the power-hungry District, Bird Power
utilizes high-voltage direct current (HVDC) cross-border interconnects to dump excess electricity onto the mainland at
below-cost wholesale rates, systematically undercutting foreign utilities and securing profound geopolitical leverage
for the District.

The utility avoids public and political pushback by keeping residential consumer rates artificially suppressed, quietly
shifting its massive revenue burden onto corporate grid tariffs and municipal infrastructure subsidies. Backed by
guaranteed statutory cash flows and unbreakable legal monopolies, BIRD equity operates as the market's premier bond
proxy. Trading with microscopic beta and flat baseline volatility, the stock ignores consumer recessions, functioning as
an indestructible, high-yielding dividend shelter for institutional portfolios during macroeconomic panics.
