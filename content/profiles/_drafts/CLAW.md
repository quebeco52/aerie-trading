## The old story

Clear Rivers Law operates as the undisputed legal juggernaut of the district. Maintaining an aggressive and sprawling
army of elite attorneys, the firm is fully equipped to handle anything from a hostile corporate takeover to a multi
billion dollar antitrust defense with just a single phone call. It has become absolute standard practice for every major
megacorporation to keep Clear Rivers heavily retained.

Operating completely without moral bias, They are notorious on Glasswater Row for pioneering a cutthroat strategy known
as litigation arbitrage. If a massive conglomerate refuses to pay their exorbitant annual retainer fees, Clear Rivers
will immediately pivot and aggressively bankroll a massive class action lawsuit on behalf of some disgruntled laid off
worker.

They ruthlessly drag out the discovery phase, weaponizing bureaucratic friction until the opposing corporate board
calculates that a massive out-of-court settlement is cheaper than fighting a multi year legal war. Naturally the firm
expertly engineers these settlements to ensure they quietly extract the lion share of the total payout.

Trading as a highly defensive asset, the stock boasts incredibly consistent cash flow from its mandatory corporate
retainers. It operates with a very stable low beta and frequently experiences sudden positive price jumps whenever a
blockbuster legal settlement is officially inked.

## The old chief executive's quote

> The law isn't blind. It just has an expensive entry fee.
