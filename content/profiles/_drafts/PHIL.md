## The old story

Pheasant and Morris International stands as a colossal titan in the production and distribution of legacy tobacco and
next generation smoke free products. Rather than apologizing for its controversial industry, the company aggressively
flexes its massive lobbying budget to crush regulatory threats and ensure its addictive products remain universally
accessible.

While its traditional cigarette brands continue to generate staggering cash flows from legacy consumers, the true
financial genius of the company lies in its aggressive pivot toward sleek new heated tobacco technologies. By rebranding
a historic vice as a pristine modern tech accessory, they have successfully hooked an entirely new generation of
stressed corporate workers.

These modern vaporizers eliminate the dirty ash of the past, replacing it with highly profitable clouds of tailored
dopamine that workers casually puff in the exact same corporate lobbies where they drink their morning coffee. Because
chemical dependency is completely immune to economic cycles, the stock remains deeply uncorrelated to the broader
market. It operates with a remarkably low beta and prints massive dividends, only suffering sharp price drops when
sudden regulatory litigation threatens their impenetrable profit margins.

## The old chief executive's quote

> Happiness is cyclical. Chemical dependency is an inelastic demand.
