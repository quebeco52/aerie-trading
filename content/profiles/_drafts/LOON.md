## The old story

Loon Call Telecom operates as an integrated telecommunications giant transmitting data, digital television, and mobile
telephony across a vast web of proprietary fiber optic infrastructure. Their network reaches deep into the most remote
and highly profitable rural and suburban markets of the District.

Their absolute dominance was achieved through scorched earth contract warfare. Whenever an ambitious tech startup
attempts to lay their own competing fiber to gain market share, Loon Call unleashes its massive capital reserves. They
aggressively bid on the exact same corporate and municipal infrastructure contracts, intentionally pricing their
services at a staggering, predatory operating loss. Loon Call will gladly bleed cash for years just to financially
suffocate a new rival. Once the upstart inevitably goes bankrupt and abandons the territory, Loon Call swoops in, buys
the half-finished infrastructure for pennies on the dollar, and immediately secures the extremely defensible revenue
stream.

Through this endless cycle of predatory bidding, they have successfully starved out every single alternative in the
District. Making them THE unavoidable telecom operator of the District.

Because citizens and businesses will prioritize paying their internet and phone bills even during the deepest
recessions, Loon Call is the ultimate defensive dividend stock. It completely ignores the frantic hype cycles of the
broader tech sector, offering investors on Glasswater Row boring, unbreakable stability and a remarkably low beta.
