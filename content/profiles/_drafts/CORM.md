## The old story

Cormorant Environmental operates as the absolute backbone of the district's sanitation and waste management. Cormorant
holds the ultimate physical monopoly over human survival in a modern city. The reality is that society is only ever one
missed garbage collection away from total collapse. If Cormorant were to suddenly halt its sprawling fleets of trucks,
the entire region would choke on its own refuse and descend into public health panic within days.

Because municipal governments can’t allow this to happen, Cormorant secures extremely lucrative and unbreakable public
contracts.

On paper, Cormorant is a publicly traded industrial firm. In reality, it operates like a generational syndicate wearing
tailored suits. The company enforces a notorious level of aggressive territorial control over landfills, recycling
centers, and private disposal sites.

Whenever a small, independent waste startup attempts to undercut Cormorant's municipal bids, those startups mysteriously
suffer catastrophic fleet fires, or their unionized drivers suffer sudden, coordinated strikes. Rumors constantly swirl
regarding the deeply entrenched, family networks that dominate Cormorant’s executive suites and regional management.
However regulators and auditors consistently look the other way. Because the streets remain immaculately clean.

For investors, this makes Cormorant the ultimate defensive stock. It churns out incredibly stable and remarkably low
volatility returns regardless of broader market panics or economic conditions, simply because humanity never stops
producing trash.
